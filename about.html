Our Bodies are NOT a Trend!

About

In a culture that says it celebrates "all bodies," we've quickly ended up with a new set of expectations. Thin bodies are often criticized or dismissed, while curvier bodies are only praised when they fit a very specific socially approved look. That isn't real progress, it's just a new version of the same old limitations.

Body positivity was never meant to highlight only certain kinds of bodies. It was meant to challenge the idea that any body needs to meet a standard in the first place. Every person, regardless of size, shape, or gender identity, deserves respect without conditions.

That belief is at the heart of everything we make. Our buttons, stickers, mugs, magnets, bags, and stationery are designed to carry that message into everyday life — tangible reminders that all bodies are valid and deserve space.
